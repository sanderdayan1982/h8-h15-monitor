Concept Guide & Macro Interpretation

What is H.8?

Fed's weekly aggregate data on commercial bank assets & liabilities. Published Fridays 16:15 ET.

Series:

Bank Credit: Total credit = securities + loans. Rising = expansionary.

Loans & Leases: Real-economy lending demand.

C&I Loans: Business investment proxy. Leading indicator.

Deposits: Funding base. Falling = funding stress.

Borrowings: Fed/FHLB borrowings. Rising = banks under pressure.

Traffic Light:

🟢 GREEN: Credit ↑, Loans ↑, Deposits ↑, Borrowings stable/↓

🟡 YELLOW: Mixed — deposits soft, borrowings creeping up

🔴 RED: Loans ↓, Deposits ↓, Borrowings ↑, C&I weak → recession risk

Concept Guide & Macro Interpretation

What is H.15?

Fed daily yields on Treasuries and key rates.

Rates:

FF (DFF): Overnight interbank rate, set by FOMC.

3M T-Bill (DTB3): Near-term expectations.

2Y (DGS2): 1-2yr rate path expectations.

10Y (DGS10): Long-term growth + inflation benchmark.

Spreads:

10Y-2Y: Negative = inverted = recession signal.

FF-3M: If FF > 3M, market prices rate cuts.

Traffic Light:

🟢 GREEN: Curve steepening, short-end easing

🟡 YELLOW: Near-zero spreads, transitional

🔴 RED: Deep inversion, FF well above 3M, restrictive

Source: Federal Reserve H.8 / H.15 via FRED API
H.8 = Weekly official release · H.15 = Delayed daily close · All data from FRED, NOT live · v2.1-AUDIT
